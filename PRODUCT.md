## Source and open decisions

This record is inferred from the existing repository content and the user’s request for three professional, trustworthy, modern law landing pages. Firm credentials, results, testimonials, fees, and any service guarantees remain unverified and must not be invented.

## Users

Primary users are Indonesian landowners, families, and property stakeholders facing a land or property dispute. They arrive looking for a clear first conversation with a qualified legal team and need to understand whether their documents, ownership, or possession position warrants action.

## Product Purpose

Pengacara Sengketa Tanah is the public-facing intake and education site for Navins Counsellors At Law. It explains land-dispute support, gives visitors a plain-language path through consultation, and turns qualified intent into a WhatsApp or email conversation.

## Positioning

The service combines document-led legal analysis with a practical path across non-litigation and litigation options for Indonesian land disputes, keeping the visitor informed about risk and next steps before escalation.

## Operating Context

Visitors often arrive under stress, from a phone, with incomplete documents and an urgent concern about ownership, possession, certificates, inheritance, or a transaction. The site is read as a first trust check before contacting the firm. It uses Indonesian legal terminology and directs conversations to WhatsApp or email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site must preserve the current navigation anchors, WhatsApp and email CTAs, FAQ behavior, article link, responsive mobile menu, accessible focus states, reduced-motion support, and modal privacy/disclaimer behavior. The page should remain lightweight and work as static files.

## Brand Commitments

The existing name is Navins Counsellors At Law / Pengacara Sengketa Tanah. The tone is professional, direct, calm, and transparent. Existing firm mark and supplied legal, document, land, and library images remain available assets.

## Product Principles

- Start with clarity about the visitor's legal concern.
- Earn trust through evidence, process, and transparent language.
- Make the first contact easy and human.
- Preserve room for both negotiated and court-led resolution.
- Keep legal information readable on small screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must retain semantic headings, skip navigation, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, descriptive image alternatives, keyboard-operable controls, and reduced-motion behavior.
